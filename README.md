# HR Analytics Dashboard – Employee Attrition Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing employee data to understand workforce distribution, employee attrition, and factors influencing employee turnover.

Using Microsoft Excel, I developed an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard with PivotTables, PivotCharts, and Slicers to explore employee information and identify attrition patterns across different categories.

The dashboard helps HR teams understand employee retention and make data-driven decisions to improve workforce management.

## Objectives

- Analyse total and active employee counts.
- Calculate employee attrition and attrition rate.
- Understand employee distribution by gender and age group.
- Analyse attrition across different education levels.
- Identify attrition patterns across departments and job roles.
- Explore employee demographics and workforce characteristics.

## Dataset Description

The dataset contains employee-related information, including:

- **Employee Information:** Employee ID, Gender, Age Group, Marital Status.
- **Job Information:** Department, Job Role, Business Travel.
- **Education:** Education Level and Education Field.
- **Employee Status:** Current Employees and Ex-Employees.
- **Compensation and Experience:** Monthly Income, Years at Company, Total Working Years.
- **Work Environment:** Job Satisfaction, Environment Satisfaction, Work-Life Balance.
- **Other Factors:** Overtime, Training, Promotions and Performance Rating.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes the following key metrics:

- **Total Employees:** Total number of employees in the dataset.
- **Active Employees:** Number of employees currently working in the organisation.
- **Attrition:** Total number of employees who have left the organisation.
- **Attrition Rate:** Percentage of employees who have left the organisation.

### Attrition Rate Calculation

Attrition Rate = (Number of Ex-Employees / Total Employees) × 100
